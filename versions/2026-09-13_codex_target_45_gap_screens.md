## Objective

Continue release/backtrack refinement against the explicit target of 45/58
correct end-to-end vehicle attributions.

## Experiments

Three controlled screens were run on the earliest supported failure groups:

1. Vehicle quarantine minimum observations `3→2` on cases 12, 23, 30, 143,
   173: zero of five confirmed events.
2. Horizontal-streak rejection disabled on cases 15, 69, 75, 152: zero of
   four confirmed events.
3. RT-DETR confidence `0.4→0.25` on detector/localization cases 18, 20, 34,
   36, 161, 193: zero of six confirmed events.

An additional case-36 screen at confidence `0.05` produced eight candidates,
seven motion-passed candidates, and zero confirmed events. This indicates
isolated low-confidence proposals without temporal evidence, not a recoverable
confidence cutoff alone.

## Ceiling and decision

The current candidate accepts events in 41/58 clips. Even a perfect reranker
would therefore cap at 41/58 until at least four additional events are
recovered. The 45/58 target requires model/data-side temporal recall plus route
improvement; release-cost tuning alone cannot reach it.

The screens are retained as negative evidence. No production default or route
cost changed. The complete NULL and fail-closed behavior remains active.

## Validation

- Each screen completed without process failure.
- Output videos were generated by the normal pipeline; no screen is an accuracy
  claim or independent-camera evaluation.
- Release identifiability focused tests and full pipeline suite remain green:
  `356 passed, 12 skipped`.
